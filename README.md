This is a physics-based platformer set in a large world where your only goal is to get your ball to the finish line. The main mechanic revolves around pressing E to split the world into two separate halves. While you control one of the balls, a second ball exists in the other half that you have no control over. Both balls have their own separate mass, meaning they react differently to momentum, gravity, and collisions as you try to guide your side safely through the map.

The level is full of traps and tricks meant to fool and troll you along the way. For instance, you cannot just jump straight over the spikes using the trampoline placed in front of them. Instead, you have to push an immovable wall toward the trampoline and hit it with your movable ball at just the right angle so it launches and lands safely behind the spikes. You can also connect two walls together using a glass bridge, though stepping onto it is risky since it can break beneath you when you press J. To add to the chaos, running into certain spikes will trigger sudden troll sound effects just to catch you off guard.

We designed and built almost everything in this project from scratch, with the exception of a few basic environment tiles. 

## Controls

To play, use W to jump, A to move backward, and D to move forward. Press E to split the world into two parts, and press J to break the glass bridge.
